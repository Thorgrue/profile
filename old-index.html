Workshops

Glorious 2030's
Through a guided utopian conversation, participants will travel to the Glorious ‘2030s, where they will collectively draw the outlines of a fairer, more sustainable and joyful society, for themselves and for the world at large. Thanks to our cards deck of initiatives, they will then decide which concrete next steps will lead to this utopia!
Draw me a future
Using a wall size poster depicting an urban-rural landscape of a society that had managed its transition, participants work in teams to elaborate the transformation of one sectors such as mobility, education or agriculture. For a playful ending, they present their programs at a fictional citizen assembly!
New narrative collage
Through a collaborative card game, participants discover the impact of stories on our brains and behaviour. In the second part, they will collectively write a story that brings about a desirable future that is compatible with planetary boundaries.

Intentions

  • strengthen cohesion and experience collective intelligence through a regenerative activity
  • articulate ideas and ideals clearly to anchor them firmly in reality
  • foster engagement with and understanding of complex issues through dialogue
  • (re)imagine individual aspirations as contributions to collective projects
  • boost collective and individual agency and reinforce the conviction that desirable futures are both possible and within reach
